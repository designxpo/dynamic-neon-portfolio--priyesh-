# Product Hunt Launch Kit — Project Cost Estimator

Everything you need to launch the **Project Cost Estimator** (`/tools/project-cost-estimator`) on Product Hunt. A good PH launch = one strong backlink from a DR-90 domain, referral traffic, and secondary pickups (newsletters, "best tools" roundups) that link you too. This is the single highest-ROI first backlink in the playbook.

> Copy-paste the drafts below, swap anything in **[brackets]**, and follow the timeline. Everything here is white-hat — **never** ask for "upvotes" (PH bans vote manipulation); ask people to "check it out."

---

## 1. Pre-launch checklist (start 1–2 weeks out)

- [ ] **Maker account** on producthunt.com with a real photo, bio, and your site link.
- [ ] **Warm up the account** — follow makers, upvote and comment genuinely for 1–2 weeks. Fresh accounts get filtered.
- [ ] **Decide: self-hunt or a hunter.** Self-hunting is completely fine today. If you know an active hunter with a following, a "Ship"/scheduled hunt helps — but don't force it.
- [ ] **Build a Teaser/"Coming soon" page** on PH (the "Ship" feature) 3–7 days ahead to collect notify-me subscribers.
- [ ] **Prep assets** (section 4).
- [ ] **Line up your network** — a list of people (email/WhatsApp/LinkedIn/X) you'll message on launch morning. Don't pre-share the direct link before it's live.
- [ ] **Pick the date** — Tues, Wed, or Thurs. Avoid weekends (low traffic) and Monday. Launch at **12:01 AM PT** (Product Hunt's day runs on Pacific time).
- [ ] **Add the PH "featured" badge** to the estimator page after launch (optional, section 8).

## 4. Media / gallery checklist

Product Hunt shows a **thumbnail** and a **gallery**. Quality here drives clicks.

- [ ] **Thumbnail** — 240×240px, simple, high-contrast (the estimator icon or a "$?" mark on brand purple).
- [ ] **Gallery image 1 (1270×760px)** — the tool in action with a real estimate showing (price range + timeline visible). This is the money shot.
- [ ] **Gallery image 2** — the feature/add-on selection (show the SEO/AEO toggles).
- [ ] **Gallery image 3** — the "shareable link + embed" moment.
- [ ] **A short GIF/video (optional but strong)** — 10–15s screen-capture of adjusting scope and the estimate updating live.
- [ ] Use the brand palette (dark `#1A152A`, purple `#5B52E0`) so it matches the destination page.

> Tip: keep text on images large and minimal — most people scan on mobile.

## 5. Launch-day timeline (all times PT)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| **12:01 AM** | Product goes live. Post your **first comment** (section 3) immediately. |
| **12:05 AM** | Message your closest 5–10 people: *"Just launched on Product Hunt — would love your honest feedback: [link]"* (never "please upvote"). |
| **6–9 AM** | Post to your LinkedIn + X with the story and link. Reply to every PH comment within minutes. |
| **Midday** | Share in relevant communities you're already part of (Indie Hackers, relevant Slacks/Discords). No spam — only where it's welcome. |
| **All day** | Reply to **every** comment. Engagement (not just votes) drives ranking. |
| **Evening** | Thank-you update in the comments; recap the feedback you got and what you'll ship next. |

## 7. Comment reply templates

- **Praise:** "Thank you! 🙏 Anything you'd want added — more project types, currencies, a PDF export?"
- **Question on accuracy:** "Great question — the ranges are ballparks tuned from real projects; the tool is honest that final pricing depends on scope. Share your estimate link and I'll send an exact quote."
- **Feature request:** "Love this idea — noting it. Would [their idea] + [related] cover your use case?"

- [ ] Log every referring domain in your backlink tracker (see the backlink playbook §8).

---

## 10. Rules & don'ts (so you don't get penalized)

- ❌ **Never** ask for "upvotes" — publicly or privately. Ask for feedback / "check it out." PH detects and penalizes vote-soliciting.
- ❌ Don't buy votes or use vote rings.
- ❌ Don't spam communities you're not part of.
- ✅ Do reply to everyone, be genuinely helpful, and be honest that it's a ballpark tool.
- ✅ Do launch once and do it well — you can't re-launch the same product repeatedly.
